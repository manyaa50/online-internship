How to Spot & Avoid Fake Internships

Cybercriminals prey on college students with deceptive postings. Our built-in Scam Detector screens every posting against these 6 primary threat vectors:

1. Upfront Payment Demands

Red Flag: The recruiter asks for a "registration fee", "security deposit", "training cost", or "laptop deposit". Real employers pay you; they never ask you to pay them.

2. Unrealistic Offers

Red Flag: Promises of "guaranteed placement", "earn ₹30,000/week", or "direct selection without interview". If an offer sounds too good to be true, it is almost certainly fraudulent.

3. Suspicious Email Domains

Red Flag: Claiming to represent a corporate brand but contacting you through free Gmail, Yahoo, or Outlook addresses instead of their official company domain (@company.com).

4. Off-Platform Chat Redirects

Red Flag: Requests like "Do not email, DM on Telegram" or "Message directly on WhatsApp to receive offer letter". Scammers use encrypted chat to evade detection.

5. Shortened & Mismatched URLs

Red Flag: Links using bit.ly, tinyurl, or unofficial blogspot pages. Authentic firms provide links directly matching their registered company website.

6. Artificial Urgency Tactics

Red Flag: "Limited seats left! Apply within 1 hour or lose your spot!". Scammers induce artificial panic to prevent you from doing background research.
